Here's where you can create and run a power schedule for your Pi. Choose when it should be on (every day, on certain days, or on a repeating cycle like 5 minutes on and 5 off) and the PV PI switches it off in between to save battery, then wakes it up again on time. With no schedule, your Pi just stays on.
